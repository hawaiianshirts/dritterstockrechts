I am not a therapist, not a doctor, not a licensed practitioner. I do not diagnose, I do not treat, and I do not replace professional mental health care.

What I offer is called coaching and conversational guidance - a quiet space, an attentive presence, a conversation without a clock or a verdict.

If I sense that you need professional therapeutic support, I will tell you directly - and help you find the right place.

What I offer

I am not a therapist - I will say that plainly. What I offer is coaching and conversational guidance. No waiting list. No insurance. No system.

Coaching

You know something needs to change - but not how. Together we find goals, paths, and the drive.

Conversational Guidance

No prescription, no judgment. An open, honest conversation - as long as you need.

Life Counselling

When everyday life gets out of hand or you have lost your direction.

Reflection Space

Sort thoughts, recognise patterns, understand yourself better.

Crisis Support

For tough phases - not a replacement for professional help, but an anchor.

Motivation Work

When drive has disappeared and you keep hitting the same wall.

Ueber mich

Ich bin in Berlin ansaessig, aufgewachsen zwischen zwei Kulturen und englischer Muttersprachler. Wenn du dich auf Englisch besser ausdruecken kannst - dann machen wir das.

Ich kenne die Denkweise und die unausgesprochenen Erwartungen, die amerikanische Klienten mitbringen. Ich stelle mich vollstaendig darauf ein. Kein gebrochenes Englisch - einfach ein Gespraech, das sich natuerlich anfuehlt.

Meine Klienten kommen aus Berlin, aus den USA und aus dem englischsprachigen Raum. Alle sind willkommen.

About me

I am based in Berlin and a native English speaker. If English is where you feel most like yourself - that is exactly how we will talk.

I understand the mindset and the unspoken expectations that American clients bring. I adapt fully. No cultural static - just a conversation that feels natural.

My clients come from Berlin, from the US, and from across the English-speaking world. Everyone is welcome.
